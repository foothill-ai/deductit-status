April 21–22, 2026: Firebase account suspension

Resolved: April 22, 2026 — 2:58 AM PDT

Starting in the early morning of April 21, our cloud hosting provider (Google Firebase) suspended the account that powers DeductIt, which took sign-in and data sync offline for roughly 24 hours. Service was restored on April 22, and no donation records were lost.

After the incident, we prioritized stronger fallback and disaster-recovery work so a single-provider disruption is less likely to take DeductIt offline at this scale again.
